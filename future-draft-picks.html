The "11*" Tradable Seconds

Technically, the Sixers control 12 tradable second-round picks. However, the 2028 Detroit 2nd is Top-55 protected, meaning it only conveys if it's one of the last 5 picks in the draft—making it a "fake" asset that is highly unlikely to ever be used.

The 2029 Swap

The 2029 1st-round pick includes "Swap Best" rights with the LA Clippers. This is Top-3 Protected, allowing the Sixers to take the better of the two picks unless the Clippers land in the Top 3.

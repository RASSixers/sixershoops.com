2023 CBA Trade Rules

Salary Matching (Non-Taxpayer)
Receive up to 200% + $250K if outgoing ≤ $7.5M; 125% + $250K if outgoing > $7.5M.
Taxpayer MLE Exception
Teams in tax range: receive up to 125% + $250K of outgoing salary.
Below Tax Team (Below LT)
Non-taxpayers can receive up to 200% + $250K if sending out ≤ $7.5M.
First Apron Teams
Cannot use Bi-Annual Exception or Non-Taxpayer MLE; can only aggregate up to 2 contracts to trade.
Second Apron Teams
Cannot aggregate contracts to match salary; cannot send cash in trades; drafted picks may not be traded if team was at/above second apron in prior year.
TPEs (Traded Player Exceptions)
Must be used within 1 year of creation. Can only absorb salary in a trade (cannot send players into a TPE trade). Max one TPE per trade per team. Cannot combine TPEs. Cannot use TPE if trading out a TPE in same deal.
No-Trade Clauses
Players with full NTC must consent. Partial NTC: player may block trade to specific teams only.
Draft Pick Trading
Stepien Rule: team must own a 1st-round pick in every other year among the next 4 drafts after the trade. Second apron teams cannot trade SSO picks (designated veteran picks).
Cash in Trades
Teams can include up to $3.75M cash per trade. Second apron teams cannot send or receive cash. Annual aggregate limit of $3.75M applies per team.
Designated Rookie Extension
Players on designated rookie extensions (supermax) cannot be traded for 6 months after signing.
Rookie Scale / Two-Way
Rookies on scale contracts can be traded after 30 days into regular season. Two-way players cannot be traded.
